# Glassbox

A read-only demo of Glassbox: a multi-tenant RAG evaluation and auto-tuning platform. Press **Try the demo** to open a workspace that already has a
document, a reviewed test set, experiments with confidence intervals, an AutoTune search, X-Ray traces and a document-change report.
Create an account to upload your own documents and ask your own questions (answers come from a free hosted model, so they can be rate limited).

The disk is erased whenever the Space restarts, so your own uploads do not persist. Source and write-up: https://github.com/samyy2323/GlassBox
