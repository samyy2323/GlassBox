# Fairview Site Handbook

## Pump inspection

If the bearing temperature of pump P-976 rises above 70 degrees, the operations manager at Fairview stops the pump and opens a work order. A pump at Fairview that fails two inspections in a row is replaced rather than repaired. Spare seals for the pumps at Fairview are kept in cabinet C-859 and the stock is counted on the first Monday of each month. The standby pump SB-132 at Fairview is run for 15 minutes every week to keep its bearings free.

## Fire drills

Fairview holds a full evacuation drill every 3 months, and the fire marshal Chen Duarte times how long the building takes to clear. Visitors at Fairview are told about the alarm test, which takes place every Monday at 10:00. Fairview has 14 trained fire wardens, one for every 30 people on site. After each drill at Fairview the marshal sends a written report to the site director within 21 days. The assembly point at Fairview is the stores building, and staff report to their warden there.

## Lockout and tagout

The energy isolation checklist at Fairview has 6 steps, and the safety officer signs it before work starts. At Fairview lockout training is repeated every 6 months and takes 12 hours. At Fairview each lockout is recorded on a red tag showing the machine number P-903, the name of the person and the time. Contractors at Fairview bring their own padlocks, which are checked at the gate by Chen Fischer. Before any repair at Fairview, the machine is isolated and locked with a personal padlock, and the key stays with the person doing the work.

## Visitor access

Visitors to Fairview sign in at the east gate and wear a badge at all times. At Fairview the visitor log is kept for 3 months and then destroyed. At Fairview a visitor may not enter the E wing without an escort from the safety officer. Visitor badges at Fairview are valid for one day and are collected when the visitor signs out. Children under 16 are not admitted to Fairview except on the open day in September.

## Chemical storage

Staff at Fairview who handle chemicals complete a 3-hour course before they get a key. Flammable liquids at Fairview may not exceed 200 litres outside the cabinet. At Fairview acids and alkalis are kept on separate shelves with at least 50 centimetres between them. A spill kit at Fairview is placed beside the door of the chemical store and checked every 21 days. At Fairview every container is labelled with the date it was opened and is discarded after 6 months. The safety data sheets for Fairview are filed by Ben Grant and a copy is posted next to cabinet C-366.

## Shift handover

If a handover at Fairview is missed, the site engineer must tell the duty manager within 8 minutes. The handover form at Fairview lists open work orders, equipment out of service and any safety incident of the last 3 hours. Night shifts at Fairview start at 07:30 and the handover form is checked by Farid Brandt the next morning. At Fairview the outgoing site engineer briefs the incoming site engineer for 8 minutes at the start of every shift. Handover forms at Fairview are scanned and kept for 12 months.

## Incident reporting

At Fairview an incident that stops production for more than 6 hours triggers a full investigation. Serious injuries at Fairview are also reported to the regulator within 30 days. At Fairview every incident is reported to Chen Eklund within 6 hours, even if nobody was hurt. At Fairview the review of an incident is led by the operations manager and finished within 30 days.

## Contractor induction

A contractor at Fairview who breaks a safety rule is removed from site for 21 days. At Fairview the induction covers the site map, the emergency exits, the lockout rules and the permit system. Contractors at Fairview receive a green helmet sticker valid for 18 months. The induction at Fairview is given by Dara Fischer on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 14:00.

## Waste disposal

At Fairview general waste, paper and metal are collected in separate bins, emptied every 7 days. The waste team at Fairview weighs the skips on the Monday of every week. Empty chemical containers at Fairview are rinsed three times before they go in the metal skip. Fairview aims to send less than 15 percent of its waste to landfill. Used oil at Fairview is stored in the drum area behind the training room and collected by a licensed carrier every 9 months.

## Emergency contacts

The first aid room at Fairview is in the east gate, and there is a trained first aider for every 15 people. At Fairview the gas supplier's emergency line is posted beside the main valve in the east gate. The duty manager at Fairview can be reached day and night on extension 3354. At Fairview the emergency number is displayed in every corridor, and the site director is Ana Brandt. At Fairview the nearest hospital is 18 kilometres away and an ambulance is expected within 15 minutes.
