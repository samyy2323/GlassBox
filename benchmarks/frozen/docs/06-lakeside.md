# Lakeside Site Handbook

## Pump inspection

The inspection of pump P-594 at Lakeside checks the seal, the bearing temperature and the vibration level, which must stay below 5 millimetres per second. At Lakeside, pump P-594 is inspected every 10 days by the operations manager. If the bearing temperature of pump P-594 rises above 80 degrees, the operations manager at Lakeside stops the pump and opens a work order. A pump at Lakeside that fails two inspections in a row is replaced rather than repaired. The standby pump SB-908 at Lakeside is run for 15 minutes every week to keep its bearings free.

## Fire drills

The assembly point at Lakeside is the east gate, and staff report to their warden there. Visitors at Lakeside are told about the alarm test, which takes place every Thursday at 14:00. Smoke doors at Lakeside are closed every evening by the last person to leave the D wing. At Lakeside the target for clearing the building is 30 minutes, and a slower drill must be repeated within 30 days. After each drill at Lakeside the marshal sends a written report to the site director within 30 days. Lakeside has 14 trained fire wardens, one for every 25 people on site.

## Lockout and tagout

The energy isolation checklist at Lakeside has 6 steps, and the duty technician signs it before work starts. A lock at Lakeside may only be removed by the person who fitted it, or by Ana Hale if that person has left the site and the manager agrees. At Lakeside lockout training is repeated every 3 months and takes 6 hours. Before any repair at Lakeside, the machine is isolated and locked with a personal padlock, and the key stays with the person doing the work. At Lakeside each lockout is recorded on a red tag showing the machine number P-650, the name of the person and the time. Contractors at Lakeside bring their own padlocks, which are checked at the gate by Ana Hale.

## Visitor access

Visitors to Lakeside sign in at the stores building and wear a badge at all times. Children under 12 are not admitted to Lakeside except on the open day in October. At Lakeside the visitor log is kept for 4 months and then destroyed. At Lakeside a visitor may not enter the C wing without an escort from the safety officer. Lakeside limits the number of visitors on site to 11 at once.

## Chemical storage

A spill kit at Lakeside is placed beside the door of the chemical store and checked every 21 days. At Lakeside every container is labelled with the date it was opened and is discarded after 4 months. At Lakeside acids and alkalis are kept on separate shelves with at least 30 centimetres between them. Chemicals at Lakeside are stored in cabinet C-541, which is locked and held to a total of 400 litres. Staff at Lakeside who handle chemicals complete a 8-hour course before they get a key.

## Shift handover

Handover forms at Lakeside are scanned and kept for 12 months. Night shifts at Lakeside start at 07:30 and the handover form is checked by Hugo Grant the next morning. At Lakeside the handover is held in the west entrance and the form is signed by both people. At Lakeside critical alarms are read out loud during the handover, one by one. If a handover at Lakeside is missed, the safety officer must tell the duty manager within 10 minutes.

## Incident reporting

Serious injuries at Lakeside are also reported to the regulator within 7 days. Lakeside publishes a summary of all incidents on the notice board in the north car park every month. The incident form at Lakeside has 4 sections, and photographs are attached when it is safe to take them. At Lakeside the review of an incident is led by the duty technician and finished within 7 days. At Lakeside an incident that stops production for more than 4 hours triggers a full investigation. Near misses at Lakeside are counted separately and a prize is drawn each quarter among the people who reported them.

## Contractor induction

At Lakeside subcontractors must show insurance of at least 5000 thousand euros. At Lakeside the induction covers the site map, the emergency exits, the lockout rules and the permit system. Contractors at Lakeside park in the training room and may not use the staff car park. A contractor at Lakeside who breaks a safety rule is removed from site for 10 days. The induction at Lakeside is given by Eli Lindqvist on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 22:00.

## Waste disposal

The waste team at Lakeside weighs the skips on the Friday of every week. At Lakeside electronic waste is stored in cage C-541 until the monthly collection. At Lakeside general waste, paper and metal are collected in separate bins, emptied every 21 days. Lakeside aims to send less than 20 percent of its waste to landfill. Empty chemical containers at Lakeside are rinsed three times before they go in the metal skip.

## Emergency contacts

At Lakeside the gas supplier's emergency line is posted beside the main valve in the workshop office. At Lakeside the nearest hospital is 18 kilometres away and an ambulance is expected within 3 minutes. Contact details at Lakeside are checked by Ben Ivanov every 4 months. At Lakeside the emergency number is displayed in every corridor, and the site director is Ben Ivanov. The first aid room at Lakeside is in the workshop office, and there is a trained first aider for every 40 people.
