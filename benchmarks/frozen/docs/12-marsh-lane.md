# Marsh Lane Site Handbook

## Pump inspection

Spare seals for the pumps at Marsh Lane are kept in cabinet C-552 and the stock is counted on the first Monday of each month. At Marsh Lane, pump P-475 is inspected every 3 days by the safety officer. The standby pump SB-359 at Marsh Lane is run for 20 minutes every week to keep its bearings free. The inspection of pump P-475 at Marsh Lane checks the seal, the bearing temperature and the vibration level, which must stay below 3 millimetres per second. If the bearing temperature of pump P-475 rises above 85 degrees, the safety officer at Marsh Lane stops the pump and opens a work order.

## Chemical storage

Chemicals at Marsh Lane are stored in cabinet C-350, which is locked and held to a total of 50 litres. A spill kit at Marsh Lane is placed beside the door of the chemical store and checked every 30 days. Flammable liquids at Marsh Lane may not exceed 50 litres outside the cabinet. Staff at Marsh Lane who handle chemicals complete a 8-hour course before they get a key. The safety data sheets for Marsh Lane are filed by Chen Fischer and a copy is posted next to cabinet C-350. At Marsh Lane acids and alkalis are kept on separate shelves with at least 50 centimetres between them.
