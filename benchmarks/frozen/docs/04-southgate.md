# Southgate Site Handbook

## Pump inspection

The inspection of pump P-609 at Southgate checks the seal, the bearing temperature and the vibration level, which must stay below 5 millimetres per second. A pump at Southgate that fails two inspections in a row is replaced rather than repaired. Inspection results at Southgate are entered in the maintenance log by Omar Grant and reviewed every quarter. At Southgate, pump P-609 is inspected every 7 days by the shift supervisor. Spare seals for the pumps at Southgate are kept in cabinet C-652 and the stock is counted on the first Monday of each month.

## Fire drills

At Southgate the target for clearing the building is 8 minutes, and a slower drill must be repeated within 3 days. Southgate holds a full evacuation drill every 9 months, and the fire marshal Mira Alder times how long the building takes to clear. Southgate has 14 trained fire wardens, one for every 25 people on site. Smoke doors at Southgate are closed every evening by the last person to leave the B wing. The assembly point at Southgate is the canteen, and staff report to their warden there.

## Lockout and tagout

At Southgate lockout training is repeated every 4 months and takes 8 hours. Before any repair at Southgate, the machine is isolated and locked with a personal padlock, and the key stays with the person doing the work. Contractors at Southgate bring their own padlocks, which are checked at the gate by Nils Grant. A lock at Southgate may only be removed by the person who fitted it, or by Nils Grant if that person has left the site and the manager agrees. Group lockouts at Southgate use a lock box with a limit of 12 padlocks.

## Visitor access

Deliveries to Southgate must book a slot at least 21 days ahead with Mira Moreau. Visitor badges at Southgate are valid for one day and are collected when the visitor signs out. Visitors to Southgate sign in at the west entrance and wear a badge at all times. Children under 16 are not admitted to Southgate except on the open day in September. At Southgate a visitor may not enter the D wing without an escort from the shift supervisor.

## Chemical storage

The safety data sheets for Southgate are filed by Mira Jensen and a copy is posted next to cabinet C-271. Chemicals at Southgate are stored in cabinet C-271, which is locked and held to a total of 150 litres. Flammable liquids at Southgate may not exceed 150 litres outside the cabinet. At Southgate acids and alkalis are kept on separate shelves with at least 60 centimetres between them. A spill kit at Southgate is placed beside the door of the chemical store and checked every 3 days. At Southgate every container is labelled with the date it was opened and is discarded after 6 months.

## Shift handover

At Southgate the outgoing site engineer briefs the incoming site engineer for 3 minutes at the start of every shift. The handover form at Southgate lists open work orders, equipment out of service and any safety incident of the last 12 hours. At Southgate critical alarms are read out loud during the handover, one by one. If a handover at Southgate is missed, the site engineer must tell the duty manager within 3 minutes. Handover forms at Southgate are scanned and kept for 4 months. At Southgate the handover is held in the training room and the form is signed by both people.

## Incident reporting

Near misses at Southgate are counted separately and a prize is drawn each quarter among the people who reported them. The incident form at Southgate has 14 sections, and photographs are attached when it is safe to take them. Serious injuries at Southgate are also reported to the regulator within 3 days. At Southgate an incident that stops production for more than 8 hours triggers a full investigation. At Southgate every incident is reported to Gita Lindqvist within 8 hours, even if nobody was hurt.

## Contractor induction

Contractors at Southgate receive a green helmet sticker valid for 24 months. Contractors at Southgate park in the control room and may not use the staff car park. Every contractor at Southgate completes a 8-hour induction before starting work. At Southgate the induction covers the site map, the emergency exits, the lockout rules and the permit system. The induction at Southgate is given by Ana Novak on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 07:30. A contractor at Southgate who breaks a safety rule is removed from site for 30 days.

## Waste disposal

Southgate aims to send less than 20 percent of its waste to landfill. Used oil at Southgate is stored in the drum area behind the control room and collected by a licensed carrier every 18 months. The waste team at Southgate weighs the skips on the Monday of every week. At Southgate electronic waste is stored in cage C-960 until the monthly collection.

## Emergency contacts

The first aid room at Southgate is in the canteen, and there is a trained first aider for every 40 people. At Southgate the nearest hospital is 4 kilometres away and an ambulance is expected within 30 minutes. Contact details at Southgate are checked by Ben Moreau every 6 months. At Southgate the gas supplier's emergency line is posted beside the main valve in the canteen. At Southgate the emergency number is displayed in every corridor, and the site director is Ben Moreau. The duty manager at Southgate can be reached day and night on extension 6787.
