# Northfield Site Handbook

## Pump inspection

A pump at Northfield that fails two inspections in a row is replaced rather than repaired. The inspection of pump P-896 at Northfield checks the seal, the bearing temperature and the vibration level, which must stay below 5 millimetres per second. At Northfield, pump P-896 is inspected every 21 days by the maintenance lead. Spare seals for the pumps at Northfield are kept in cabinet C-164 and the stock is counted on the first Monday of each month. If the bearing temperature of pump P-896 rises above 70 degrees, the maintenance lead at Northfield stops the pump and opens a work order. Inspection results at Northfield are entered in the maintenance log by Omar Castillo and reviewed every quarter.

## Fire drills

Smoke doors at Northfield are closed every evening by the last person to leave the E wing. Northfield holds a full evacuation drill every 6 months, and the fire marshal Jonas Brandt times how long the building takes to clear. The assembly point at Northfield is the main reception, and staff report to their warden there. Northfield has 12 trained fire wardens, one for every 25 people on site. At Northfield the target for clearing the building is 3 minutes, and a slower drill must be repeated within 21 days. Visitors at Northfield are told about the alarm test, which takes place every Wednesday at 14:00.

## Lockout and tagout

Contractors at Northfield bring their own padlocks, which are checked at the gate by Eli Hale. A lock at Northfield may only be removed by the person who fitted it, or by Eli Hale if that person has left the site and the manager agrees. Before any repair at Northfield, the machine is isolated and locked with a personal padlock, and the key stays with the person doing the work. At Northfield each lockout is recorded on a red tag showing the machine number P-596, the name of the person and the time. At Northfield lockout training is repeated every 18 months and takes 3 hours.

## Visitor access

Visitors to Northfield sign in at the stores building and wear a badge at all times. Children under 12 are not admitted to Northfield except on the open day in March. At Northfield a visitor may not enter the A wing without an escort from the safety officer. Visitor badges at Northfield are valid for one day and are collected when the visitor signs out. At Northfield the visitor log is kept for 9 months and then destroyed.

## Chemical storage

Staff at Northfield who handle chemicals complete a 12-hour course before they get a key. Flammable liquids at Northfield may not exceed 150 litres outside the cabinet. At Northfield every container is labelled with the date it was opened and is discarded after 18 months. A spill kit at Northfield is placed beside the door of the chemical store and checked every 3 days. Chemicals at Northfield are stored in cabinet C-153, which is locked and held to a total of 150 litres.

## Shift handover

The handover form at Northfield lists open work orders, equipment out of service and any safety incident of the last 2 hours. At Northfield the handover is held in the training room and the form is signed by both people. At Northfield critical alarms are read out loud during the handover, one by one. Handover forms at Northfield are scanned and kept for 3 months. At Northfield the outgoing site engineer briefs the incoming site engineer for 30 minutes at the start of every shift.

## Incident reporting

At Northfield the review of an incident is led by the operations manager and finished within 30 days. Northfield publishes a summary of all incidents on the notice board in the stores building every month. Serious injuries at Northfield are also reported to the regulator within 30 days. Near misses at Northfield are counted separately and a prize is drawn each quarter among the people who reported them. At Northfield every incident is reported to Pia Hale within 8 hours, even if nobody was hurt. At Northfield an incident that stops production for more than 8 hours triggers a full investigation.

## Contractor induction

Contractors at Northfield park in the canteen and may not use the staff car park. A contractor at Northfield who breaks a safety rule is removed from site for 10 days. At Northfield subcontractors must show insurance of at least 750 thousand euros. The induction at Northfield is given by Eli Hale on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 09:00. Contractors at Northfield receive a green helmet sticker valid for 9 months. Every contractor at Northfield completes a 2-hour induction before starting work.

## Waste disposal

The waste team at Northfield weighs the skips on the Friday of every week. At Northfield hazardous waste is logged by Jonas Lindqvist on a consignment note before it leaves the site. Used oil at Northfield is stored in the drum area behind the north car park and collected by a licensed carrier every 3 months. Northfield aims to send less than 5 percent of its waste to landfill. At Northfield general waste, paper and metal are collected in separate bins, emptied every 30 days.

## Emergency contacts

Contact details at Northfield are checked by Ines Moreau every 3 months. At Northfield the nearest hospital is 18 kilometres away and an ambulance is expected within 5 minutes. The first aid room at Northfield is in the main reception, and there is a trained first aider for every 20 people. At Northfield the gas supplier's emergency line is posted beside the main valve in the main reception. Northfield runs an emergency call test every Thursday at 22:00. At Northfield the emergency number is displayed in every corridor, and the site director is Ines Moreau.
