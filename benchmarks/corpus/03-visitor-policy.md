# Visitor and Contractor Policy

## Who this policy covers
This policy applies to everyone who enters the Harbour Road campus without an employee badge, including customers, job candidates, delivery drivers and contractors. Employees who host a visitor are fully responsible for that visitor from arrival until departure.

## Advance registration
Hosts must register expected visitors in the reception system by 16:00 on the working day before the visit. A registered visitor receives an email with the address, parking instructions and the start time. Unregistered visitors can still be admitted, but they wait at reception until the host confirms them in person.

## Signing in
Every visitor signs in at the front desk, shows a photo identity document and receives a printed badge with the host's name and the date. Identity documents are checked but never copied or kept. Badges must be worn on the upper body and clearly visible for the entire visit.

## Escorting
Visitors must be escorted by their host or a named colleague at all times. They may not walk alone between rooms, use the lifts without an escort, or enter laboratories, the server room or the finance office. A visitor found alone is politely stopped and taken back to reception by the nearest employee.

## Visitor badge colours
White badges are for short visits of up to four hours, yellow badges for full-day visits and green badges for contractors with a valid work permit. Badges expire at midnight and are collected at the front desk on departure. Staff who see a badge from the previous day should report it to security at once.

## Contractor requirements
Contractors need a signed work permit, proof of insurance covering at least two million pounds, and a completed site induction before they start work. The induction takes forty minutes and is repeated every twelve months. Contractors working with hot tools or at height need an additional permit from the facilities manager.

## Parking
Visitor parking is available in the lower car park, with twenty marked bays. Hosts may reserve a bay through reception. Vehicles without a permit are limited to four hours, and the campus is not responsible for loss or damage to vehicles or their contents.

## Photography and recording
Taking photographs or video on site is not allowed without written permission from the communications team. Visitors are asked to leave phones in their pockets inside production areas. Meeting rooms displaying a red sign are in use for confidential discussions, and no recording of any kind is permitted there.

## Network access
Visitors may use the guest wireless network, which is separate from the corporate network and gives internet access only. A new password is issued each morning at reception. Visitors may not plug any device into a wall socket marked with a network label, and contractors needing network access must request it through the IT service desk.

## Deliveries
Couriers deliver to the loading bay, not to the front desk, between 08:00 and 17:00. The goods-in clerk checks each delivery against the order and signs the courier's sheet. Large deliveries over 25 kilograms must be booked in advance so that the forklift and a second person are available.

## Emergencies and visitors
In an alarm the host leads the visitor to the nearest assembly point and reports to the fire warden. Reception keeps a live list of badges issued, and the security officer uses it for the roll call. Visitors must never use the lifts during an evacuation.

## Incidents and complaints
Any theft, injury or unsafe behaviour involving a visitor is reported to security immediately and recorded in the incident book. Complaints about the visitor experience go to the office manager, who replies within five working days. Serious breaches of this policy may lead to a visitor being banned from the campus.
