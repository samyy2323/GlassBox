# Eastport Site Handbook

## Pump inspection

A pump at Eastport that fails two inspections in a row is replaced rather than repaired. Spare seals for the pumps at Eastport are kept in cabinet C-486 and the stock is counted on the first Monday of each month. The inspection of pump P-915 at Eastport checks the seal, the bearing temperature and the vibration level, which must stay below 2 millimetres per second. At Eastport, pump P-915 is inspected every 30 days by the shift supervisor.

## Fire drills

Visitors at Eastport are told about the alarm test, which takes place every Tuesday at 06:00. Eastport holds a full evacuation drill every 6 months, and the fire marshal Mira Brandt times how long the building takes to clear. After each drill at Eastport the marshal sends a written report to the site director within 10 days. The assembly point at Eastport is the canteen, and staff report to their warden there. At Eastport the target for clearing the building is 8 minutes, and a slower drill must be repeated within 10 days.

## Lockout and tagout

The energy isolation checklist at Eastport has 11 steps, and the duty technician signs it before work starts. Before any repair at Eastport, the machine is isolated and locked with a personal padlock, and the key stays with the person doing the work. Group lockouts at Eastport use a lock box with a limit of 11 padlocks. At Eastport each lockout is recorded on a red tag showing the machine number P-286, the name of the person and the time. At Eastport lockout training is repeated every 3 months and takes 12 hours.

## Visitor access

Visitors to Eastport sign in at the workshop office and wear a badge at all times. At Eastport a visitor may not enter the C wing without an escort from the operations manager. Visitor badges at Eastport are valid for one day and are collected when the visitor signs out. Deliveries to Eastport must book a slot at least 30 days ahead with Chen Jensen. Children under 18 are not admitted to Eastport except on the open day in June.

## Chemical storage

Flammable liquids at Eastport may not exceed 50 litres outside the cabinet. Chemicals at Eastport are stored in cabinet C-165, which is locked and held to a total of 50 litres. The safety data sheets for Eastport are filed by Gita Ivanov and a copy is posted next to cabinet C-165. Staff at Eastport who handle chemicals complete a 4-hour course before they get a key. A spill kit at Eastport is placed beside the door of the chemical store and checked every 3 days.

## Shift handover

If a handover at Eastport is missed, the duty technician must tell the duty manager within 10 minutes. The handover form at Eastport lists open work orders, equipment out of service and any safety incident of the last 8 hours. Handover forms at Eastport are scanned and kept for 18 months. At Eastport critical alarms are read out loud during the handover, one by one. Night shifts at Eastport start at 07:30 and the handover form is checked by Ines Eklund the next morning.

## Incident reporting

Near misses at Eastport are counted separately and a prize is drawn each quarter among the people who reported them. At Eastport an incident that stops production for more than 12 hours triggers a full investigation. At Eastport the review of an incident is led by the duty technician and finished within 10 days. The incident form at Eastport has 4 sections, and photographs are attached when it is safe to take them. Serious injuries at Eastport are also reported to the regulator within 10 days.

## Contractor induction

Every contractor at Eastport completes a 12-hour induction before starting work. At Eastport subcontractors must show insurance of at least 500 thousand euros. Contractors at Eastport receive a green helmet sticker valid for 6 months. Contractors at Eastport park in the training room and may not use the staff car park. A contractor at Eastport who breaks a safety rule is removed from site for 3 days.

## Waste disposal

The waste team at Eastport weighs the skips on the Thursday of every week. At Eastport hazardous waste is logged by Jonas Jensen on a consignment note before it leaves the site. Used oil at Eastport is stored in the drum area behind the canteen and collected by a licensed carrier every 3 months. At Eastport general waste, paper and metal are collected in separate bins, emptied every 7 days. Empty chemical containers at Eastport are rinsed three times before they go in the metal skip. Eastport aims to send less than 30 percent of its waste to landfill.

## Emergency contacts

At Eastport the emergency number is displayed in every corridor, and the site director is Pia Ivanov. The first aid room at Eastport is in the north car park, and there is a trained first aider for every 40 people. At Eastport the gas supplier's emergency line is posted beside the main valve in the north car park. Contact details at Eastport are checked by Pia Ivanov every 4 months. Eastport runs an emergency call test every Monday at 09:00. The duty manager at Eastport can be reached day and night on extension 9230.
