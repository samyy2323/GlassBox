# Oakhurst Site Handbook

## Pump inspection

The standby pump SB-112 at Oakhurst is run for 30 minutes every week to keep its bearings free. Inspection results at Oakhurst are entered in the maintenance log by Farid Ivanov and reviewed every quarter. If the bearing temperature of pump P-284 rises above 75 degrees, the maintenance lead at Oakhurst stops the pump and opens a work order. The inspection of pump P-284 at Oakhurst checks the seal, the bearing temperature and the vibration level, which must stay below 7 millimetres per second. At Oakhurst, pump P-284 is inspected every 10 days by the maintenance lead.

## Fire drills

After each drill at Oakhurst the marshal sends a written report to the site director within 10 days. The assembly point at Oakhurst is the east gate, and staff report to their warden there. Oakhurst holds a full evacuation drill every 6 months, and the fire marshal Farid Hale times how long the building takes to clear. Visitors at Oakhurst are told about the alarm test, which takes place every Thursday at 09:00. Oakhurst has 8 trained fire wardens, one for every 20 people on site.

## Lockout and tagout

Contractors at Oakhurst bring their own padlocks, which are checked at the gate by Ines Brandt. At Oakhurst lockout training is repeated every 12 months and takes 6 hours. Group lockouts at Oakhurst use a lock box with a limit of 8 padlocks. A lock at Oakhurst may only be removed by the person who fitted it, or by Ines Brandt if that person has left the site and the manager agrees. Before any repair at Oakhurst, the machine is isolated and locked with a personal padlock, and the key stays with the person doing the work.

## Visitor access

Deliveries to Oakhurst must book a slot at least 7 days ahead with Ben Hale. At Oakhurst the visitor log is kept for 3 months and then destroyed. Oakhurst limits the number of visitors on site to 4 at once. At Oakhurst a visitor may not enter the A wing without an escort from the site engineer.

## Chemical storage

Staff at Oakhurst who handle chemicals complete a 12-hour course before they get a key. Chemicals at Oakhurst are stored in cabinet C-489, which is locked and held to a total of 100 litres. Flammable liquids at Oakhurst may not exceed 100 litres outside the cabinet. At Oakhurst every container is labelled with the date it was opened and is discarded after 4 months.

## Shift handover

At Oakhurst the outgoing operations manager briefs the incoming operations manager for 3 minutes at the start of every shift. The handover form at Oakhurst lists open work orders, equipment out of service and any safety incident of the last 8 hours. Night shifts at Oakhurst start at 07:30 and the handover form is checked by Luca Fischer the next morning. If a handover at Oakhurst is missed, the operations manager must tell the duty manager within 3 minutes.

## Incident reporting

Oakhurst publishes a summary of all incidents on the notice board in the training room every month. At Oakhurst every incident is reported to Ana Keller within 8 hours, even if nobody was hurt. The incident form at Oakhurst has 11 sections, and photographs are attached when it is safe to take them. At Oakhurst the review of an incident is led by the duty technician and finished within 7 days. Serious injuries at Oakhurst are also reported to the regulator within 7 days.

## Contractor induction

At Oakhurst the induction covers the site map, the emergency exits, the lockout rules and the permit system. The induction at Oakhurst is given by Nils Castillo on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 10:00. At Oakhurst subcontractors must show insurance of at least 750 thousand euros. A contractor at Oakhurst who breaks a safety rule is removed from site for 7 days. Contractors at Oakhurst receive a green helmet sticker valid for 24 months. Every contractor at Oakhurst completes a 2-hour induction before starting work.

## Waste disposal

Oakhurst aims to send less than 10 percent of its waste to landfill. At Oakhurst general waste, paper and metal are collected in separate bins, emptied every 7 days. At Oakhurst hazardous waste is logged by Jonas Ivanov on a consignment note before it leaves the site. At Oakhurst electronic waste is stored in cage C-794 until the monthly collection. Used oil at Oakhurst is stored in the drum area behind the west entrance and collected by a licensed carrier every 18 months.

## Emergency contacts

Oakhurst runs an emergency call test every Friday at 09:00. The duty manager at Oakhurst can be reached day and night on extension 8262. At Oakhurst the emergency number is displayed in every corridor, and the site director is Luca Castillo. The first aid room at Oakhurst is in the training room, and there is a trained first aider for every 20 people.
