# Riverside Site Handbook

## Pump inspection

Inspection results at Riverside are entered in the maintenance log by Nils Grant and reviewed every quarter. At Riverside, pump P-787 is inspected every 30 days by the shift supervisor. A pump at Riverside that fails two inspections in a row is replaced rather than repaired. If the bearing temperature of pump P-787 rises above 60 degrees, the shift supervisor at Riverside stops the pump and opens a work order. Spare seals for the pumps at Riverside are kept in cabinet C-520 and the stock is counted on the first Monday of each month.

## Fire drills

At Riverside the target for clearing the building is 3 minutes, and a slower drill must be repeated within 5 days. Visitors at Riverside are told about the alarm test, which takes place every Monday at 14:00. Smoke doors at Riverside are closed every evening by the last person to leave the A wing. The assembly point at Riverside is the main reception, and staff report to their warden there. Riverside holds a full evacuation drill every 18 months, and the fire marshal Gita Grant times how long the building takes to clear.

## Lockout and tagout

A lock at Riverside may only be removed by the person who fitted it, or by Ben Fischer if that person has left the site and the manager agrees. Contractors at Riverside bring their own padlocks, which are checked at the gate by Ben Fischer. The energy isolation checklist at Riverside has 6 steps, and the duty technician signs it before work starts. Before any repair at Riverside, the machine is isolated and locked with a personal padlock, and the key stays with the person doing the work.

## Visitor access

Visitor badges at Riverside are valid for one day and are collected when the visitor signs out. Riverside limits the number of visitors on site to 12 at once. At Riverside a visitor may not enter the A wing without an escort from the operations manager. Visitors to Riverside sign in at the stores building and wear a badge at all times. At Riverside the visitor log is kept for 12 months and then destroyed.

## Chemical storage

A spill kit at Riverside is placed beside the door of the chemical store and checked every 21 days. At Riverside acids and alkalis are kept on separate shelves with at least 100 centimetres between them. Flammable liquids at Riverside may not exceed 100 litres outside the cabinet. Staff at Riverside who handle chemicals complete a 8-hour course before they get a key. The safety data sheets for Riverside are filed by Farid Alder and a copy is posted next to cabinet C-860. At Riverside every container is labelled with the date it was opened and is discarded after 18 months.

## Shift handover

If a handover at Riverside is missed, the duty technician must tell the duty manager within 20 minutes. Handover forms at Riverside are scanned and kept for 12 months. At Riverside the handover is held in the east gate and the form is signed by both people. Night shifts at Riverside start at 09:00 and the handover form is checked by Pia Brandt the next morning. The handover form at Riverside lists open work orders, equipment out of service and any safety incident of the last 6 hours. At Riverside critical alarms are read out loud during the handover, one by one.

## Incident reporting

The incident form at Riverside has 14 sections, and photographs are attached when it is safe to take them. Near misses at Riverside are counted separately and a prize is drawn each quarter among the people who reported them. Riverside publishes a summary of all incidents on the notice board in the training room every month. At Riverside every incident is reported to Pia Duarte within 8 hours, even if nobody was hurt.

## Contractor induction

At Riverside subcontractors must show insurance of at least 5000 thousand euros. The induction at Riverside is given by Jonas Moreau on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 06:00. A contractor at Riverside who breaks a safety rule is removed from site for 10 days. Contractors at Riverside park in the east gate and may not use the staff car park.

## Waste disposal

At Riverside electronic waste is stored in cage C-639 until the monthly collection. At Riverside general waste, paper and metal are collected in separate bins, emptied every 21 days. Used oil at Riverside is stored in the drum area behind the west entrance and collected by a licensed carrier every 4 months. At Riverside hazardous waste is logged by Mira Alder on a consignment note before it leaves the site. Empty chemical containers at Riverside are rinsed three times before they go in the metal skip. The waste team at Riverside weighs the skips on the Thursday of every week.
