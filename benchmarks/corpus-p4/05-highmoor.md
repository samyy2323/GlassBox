# Highmoor Site Handbook

## Pump inspection

Spare seals for the pumps at Highmoor are kept in cabinet C-785 and the stock is counted on the first Monday of each month. If the bearing temperature of pump P-531 rises above 85 degrees, the operations manager at Highmoor stops the pump and opens a work order. At Highmoor, pump P-531 is inspected every 21 days by the operations manager. Inspection results at Highmoor are entered in the maintenance log by Gita Moreau and reviewed every quarter. The inspection of pump P-531 at Highmoor checks the seal, the bearing temperature and the vibration level, which must stay below 7 millimetres per second.

## Fire drills

At Highmoor the target for clearing the building is 30 minutes, and a slower drill must be repeated within 21 days. Highmoor has 8 trained fire wardens, one for every 30 people on site. Smoke doors at Highmoor are closed every evening by the last person to leave the B wing. Visitors at Highmoor are told about the alarm test, which takes place every Monday at 22:00. Highmoor holds a full evacuation drill every 18 months, and the fire marshal Hugo Alder times how long the building takes to clear.

## Lockout and tagout

Group lockouts at Highmoor use a lock box with a limit of 12 padlocks. Contractors at Highmoor bring their own padlocks, which are checked at the gate by Kira Castillo. At Highmoor each lockout is recorded on a red tag showing the machine number P-574, the name of the person and the time. At Highmoor lockout training is repeated every 9 months and takes 12 hours. A lock at Highmoor may only be removed by the person who fitted it, or by Kira Castillo if that person has left the site and the manager agrees.

## Visitor access

At Highmoor a visitor may not enter the F wing without an escort from the operations manager. At Highmoor the visitor log is kept for 18 months and then destroyed. Visitor badges at Highmoor are valid for one day and are collected when the visitor signs out. Highmoor limits the number of visitors on site to 9 at once.

## Chemical storage

At Highmoor every container is labelled with the date it was opened and is discarded after 12 months. The safety data sheets for Highmoor are filed by Luca Grant and a copy is posted next to cabinet C-341. At Highmoor acids and alkalis are kept on separate shelves with at least 60 centimetres between them. Staff at Highmoor who handle chemicals complete a 12-hour course before they get a key. A spill kit at Highmoor is placed beside the door of the chemical store and checked every 3 days. Chemicals at Highmoor are stored in cabinet C-341, which is locked and held to a total of 250 litres.

## Shift handover

If a handover at Highmoor is missed, the duty technician must tell the duty manager within 8 minutes. Night shifts at Highmoor start at 07:30 and the handover form is checked by Gita Brandt the next morning. At Highmoor the outgoing duty technician briefs the incoming duty technician for 8 minutes at the start of every shift. At Highmoor the handover is held in the north car park and the form is signed by both people. The handover form at Highmoor lists open work orders, equipment out of service and any safety incident of the last 8 hours.

## Incident reporting

At Highmoor every incident is reported to Omar Duarte within 8 hours, even if nobody was hurt. At Highmoor the review of an incident is led by the duty technician and finished within 30 days. The incident form at Highmoor has 6 sections, and photographs are attached when it is safe to take them. At Highmoor an incident that stops production for more than 8 hours triggers a full investigation. Highmoor publishes a summary of all incidents on the notice board in the loading bay every month.

## Contractor induction

Contractors at Highmoor receive a green helmet sticker valid for 18 months. At Highmoor the induction covers the site map, the emergency exits, the lockout rules and the permit system. A contractor at Highmoor who breaks a safety rule is removed from site for 21 days. The induction at Highmoor is given by Eli Lindqvist on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 07:30. Contractors at Highmoor park in the control room and may not use the staff car park.

## Waste disposal

Used oil at Highmoor is stored in the drum area behind the stores building and collected by a licensed carrier every 12 months. Highmoor aims to send less than 30 percent of its waste to landfill. The waste team at Highmoor weighs the skips on the Tuesday of every week. At Highmoor general waste, paper and metal are collected in separate bins, emptied every 7 days. At Highmoor electronic waste is stored in cage C-120 until the monthly collection. At Highmoor hazardous waste is logged by Ben Hale on a consignment note before it leaves the site.

## Emergency contacts

Highmoor runs an emergency call test every Thursday at 14:00. At Highmoor the gas supplier's emergency line is posted beside the main valve in the loading bay. The duty manager at Highmoor can be reached day and night on extension 5380. At Highmoor the emergency number is displayed in every corridor, and the site director is Kira Lindqvist. At Highmoor the nearest hospital is 4 kilometres away and an ambulance is expected within 5 minutes.
