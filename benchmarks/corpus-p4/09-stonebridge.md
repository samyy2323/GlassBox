# Stonebridge Site Handbook

## Pump inspection

If the bearing temperature of pump P-632 rises above 60 degrees, the maintenance lead at Stonebridge stops the pump and opens a work order. At Stonebridge, pump P-632 is inspected every 7 days by the maintenance lead. A pump at Stonebridge that fails two inspections in a row is replaced rather than repaired. The inspection of pump P-632 at Stonebridge checks the seal, the bearing temperature and the vibration level, which must stay below 2 millimetres per second. Inspection results at Stonebridge are entered in the maintenance log by Eli Novak and reviewed every quarter. Spare seals for the pumps at Stonebridge are kept in cabinet C-777 and the stock is counted on the first Monday of each month.

## Fire drills

Stonebridge has 8 trained fire wardens, one for every 30 people on site. At Stonebridge the target for clearing the building is 30 minutes, and a slower drill must be repeated within 30 days. The assembly point at Stonebridge is the training room, and staff report to their warden there. Stonebridge holds a full evacuation drill every 4 months, and the fire marshal Jonas Fischer times how long the building takes to clear. Smoke doors at Stonebridge are closed every evening by the last person to leave the F wing.

## Lockout and tagout

Group lockouts at Stonebridge use a lock box with a limit of 4 padlocks. Before any repair at Stonebridge, the machine is isolated and locked with a personal padlock, and the key stays with the person doing the work. At Stonebridge each lockout is recorded on a red tag showing the machine number P-611, the name of the person and the time. At Stonebridge lockout training is repeated every 3 months and takes 2 hours. A lock at Stonebridge may only be removed by the person who fitted it, or by Hugo Ivanov if that person has left the site and the manager agrees.

## Visitor access

Deliveries to Stonebridge must book a slot at least 3 days ahead with Eli Alder. At Stonebridge the visitor log is kept for 18 months and then destroyed. At Stonebridge a visitor may not enter the E wing without an escort from the maintenance lead. Stonebridge limits the number of visitors on site to 6 at once. Children under 16 are not admitted to Stonebridge except on the open day in March.

## Chemical storage

Staff at Stonebridge who handle chemicals complete a 2-hour course before they get a key. A spill kit at Stonebridge is placed beside the door of the chemical store and checked every 14 days. Flammable liquids at Stonebridge may not exceed 250 litres outside the cabinet. The safety data sheets for Stonebridge are filed by Dara Duarte and a copy is posted next to cabinet C-538.

## Shift handover

At Stonebridge critical alarms are read out loud during the handover, one by one. If a handover at Stonebridge is missed, the safety officer must tell the duty manager within 5 minutes. At Stonebridge the outgoing safety officer briefs the incoming safety officer for 5 minutes at the start of every shift. At Stonebridge the handover is held in the workshop office and the form is signed by both people. Handover forms at Stonebridge are scanned and kept for 6 months. Night shifts at Stonebridge start at 14:00 and the handover form is checked by Chen Castillo the next morning.

## Incident reporting

Serious injuries at Stonebridge are also reported to the regulator within 30 days. At Stonebridge every incident is reported to Ben Ivanov within 3 hours, even if nobody was hurt. At Stonebridge the review of an incident is led by the maintenance lead and finished within 30 days. Near misses at Stonebridge are counted separately and a prize is drawn each quarter among the people who reported them.

## Contractor induction

At Stonebridge the induction covers the site map, the emergency exits, the lockout rules and the permit system. Contractors at Stonebridge park in the main reception and may not use the staff car park. The induction at Stonebridge is given by Dara Alder on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 10:00. At Stonebridge subcontractors must show insurance of at least 2000 thousand euros.

## Waste disposal

Empty chemical containers at Stonebridge are rinsed three times before they go in the metal skip. The waste team at Stonebridge weighs the skips on the Thursday of every week. At Stonebridge general waste, paper and metal are collected in separate bins, emptied every 30 days. At Stonebridge electronic waste is stored in cage C-212 until the monthly collection. At Stonebridge hazardous waste is logged by Eli Moreau on a consignment note before it leaves the site. Stonebridge aims to send less than 10 percent of its waste to landfill.

## Emergency contacts

Contact details at Stonebridge are checked by Omar Keller every 12 months. The first aid room at Stonebridge is in the west entrance, and there is a trained first aider for every 15 people. At Stonebridge the nearest hospital is 4 kilometres away and an ambulance is expected within 30 minutes. Stonebridge runs an emergency call test every Monday at 22:00.
