At Westbrook acids and alkalis are kept on separate shelves with at least 100 centimetres between them. A spill kit at Westbrook is placed beside the door of the chemical store and checked every 21 days. Chemicals at Westbrook are stored in cabinet C-353, which is locked and held to a total of 150 litres.
