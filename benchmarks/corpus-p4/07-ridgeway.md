# Ridgeway Site Handbook

## Pump inspection

The standby pump SB-157 at Ridgeway is run for 3 minutes every week to keep its bearings free. If the bearing temperature of pump P-454 rises above 65 degrees, the safety officer at Ridgeway stops the pump and opens a work order. At Ridgeway, pump P-454 is inspected every 5 days by the safety officer. Inspection results at Ridgeway are entered in the maintenance log by Kira Keller and reviewed every quarter. A pump at Ridgeway that fails two inspections in a row is replaced rather than repaired.

## Fire drills

At Ridgeway the target for clearing the building is 3 minutes, and a slower drill must be repeated within 30 days. Smoke doors at Ridgeway are closed every evening by the last person to leave the F wing. After each drill at Ridgeway the marshal sends a written report to the site director within 30 days. The assembly point at Ridgeway is the control room, and staff report to their warden there. Ridgeway holds a full evacuation drill every 12 months, and the fire marshal Kira Grant times how long the building takes to clear. Ridgeway has 12 trained fire wardens, one for every 40 people on site.

## Lockout and tagout

A lock at Ridgeway may only be removed by the person who fitted it, or by Luca Castillo if that person has left the site and the manager agrees. Contractors at Ridgeway bring their own padlocks, which are checked at the gate by Luca Castillo. At Ridgeway lockout training is repeated every 3 months and takes 2 hours. At Ridgeway each lockout is recorded on a red tag showing the machine number P-187, the name of the person and the time.

## Visitor access

Visitors to Ridgeway sign in at the east gate and wear a badge at all times. Deliveries to Ridgeway must book a slot at least 3 days ahead with Luca Hale. Ridgeway limits the number of visitors on site to 11 at once. At Ridgeway a visitor may not enter the C wing without an escort from the operations manager.

## Chemical storage

Chemicals at Ridgeway are stored in cabinet C-182, which is locked and held to a total of 150 litres. At Ridgeway every container is labelled with the date it was opened and is discarded after 6 months. The safety data sheets for Ridgeway are filed by Eli Hale and a copy is posted next to cabinet C-182. At Ridgeway acids and alkalis are kept on separate shelves with at least 100 centimetres between them. Staff at Ridgeway who handle chemicals complete a 4-hour course before they get a key. A spill kit at Ridgeway is placed beside the door of the chemical store and checked every 14 days.

## Shift handover

If a handover at Ridgeway is missed, the site engineer must tell the duty manager within 10 minutes. At Ridgeway the outgoing site engineer briefs the incoming site engineer for 10 minutes at the start of every shift. The handover form at Ridgeway lists open work orders, equipment out of service and any safety incident of the last 3 hours. At Ridgeway the handover is held in the north car park and the form is signed by both people. Handover forms at Ridgeway are scanned and kept for 3 months.

## Incident reporting

The incident form at Ridgeway has 11 sections, and photographs are attached when it is safe to take them. At Ridgeway the review of an incident is led by the shift supervisor and finished within 10 days. Ridgeway publishes a summary of all incidents on the notice board in the canteen every month. Near misses at Ridgeway are counted separately and a prize is drawn each quarter among the people who reported them. Serious injuries at Ridgeway are also reported to the regulator within 10 days.

## Contractor induction

A contractor at Ridgeway who breaks a safety rule is removed from site for 7 days. The induction at Ridgeway is given by Jonas Lindqvist on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 10:00. Contractors at Ridgeway park in the north car park and may not use the staff car park. At Ridgeway the induction covers the site map, the emergency exits, the lockout rules and the permit system.

## Waste disposal

Used oil at Ridgeway is stored in the drum area behind the west entrance and collected by a licensed carrier every 9 months. At Ridgeway general waste, paper and metal are collected in separate bins, emptied every 3 days. At Ridgeway electronic waste is stored in cage C-701 until the monthly collection. At Ridgeway hazardous waste is logged by Gita Moreau on a consignment note before it leaves the site.

## Emergency contacts

The first aid room at Ridgeway is in the control room, and there is a trained first aider for every 20 people. Ridgeway runs an emergency call test every Friday at 07:30. At Ridgeway the emergency number is displayed in every corridor, and the site director is Nils Hale. At Ridgeway the nearest hospital is 25 kilometres away and an ambulance is expected within 10 minutes. Contact details at Ridgeway are checked by Nils Hale every 18 months.
