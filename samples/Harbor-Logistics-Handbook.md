# Harbor Logistics Employee Handbook

Edition 1, effective 1 March 2026. This handbook applies to all employees of Harbor Logistics Ltd at the Port Merrow depot and the Eastgate warehouse. It is maintained by the People team, led by Director of People Imelda Okafor.

## 1. Working hours

The standard working week at Harbor Logistics is 40 hours, from Monday to Friday. The depot opens at 06:00 and closes at 22:00. Employees on the early shift work from 06:00 to 14:00, and employees on the late shift work from 14:00 to 22:00. A 30-minute unpaid break is taken by every employee who works more than 6 hours. Overtime is paid at 1.5 times the normal hourly rate for the first 10 overtime hours in a week, and at 2 times the normal hourly rate after that. Overtime must be approved in advance by a shift supervisor.

## 2. Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives 20 days of paid annual leave per year, in addition to the 9 public holidays. Leave is counted from 1 January to 31 December. Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused days into the next year, and carried-over days must be used by 31 March. Leave requests must be submitted at least 14 days in advance through the People portal, and the shift supervisor answers within 3 working days. During the peak season, from 1 November to 24 December, no more than 2 employees per shift may be on leave at the same time.

## 3. Sick leave

An employee who cannot come to work must telephone their shift supervisor before the start of their shift, and no later than 05:30 for the early shift. A medical certificate is required from the 3rd consecutive day of absence. Sick leave is paid in full for the first 10 days in a calendar year and at 60 percent of normal pay for the following 20 days. Sick leave does not reduce annual leave.

## 4. Safety and personal protective equipment

Safety boots, a high-visibility vest and a hard hat are mandatory in the warehouse and on the quayside. Hearing protection is mandatory in the container yard, where noise exceeds 85 decibels. The safety officer at Port Merrow is Tomas Brandt, and the safety officer at Eastgate is Lena Vogel. Protective equipment is issued free of charge and replaced when damaged, and a replacement pair of safety boots is issued every 12 months.

## 5. Forklift certification

Only employees with a valid forklift licence may operate a forklift. A forklift licence is valid for 3 years. Renewal training takes one day and is organised by the safety officer. The maximum speed of a forklift inside the warehouse is 8 km/h, and the maximum speed in the container yard is 15 km/h. A forklift must never carry a load that blocks the driver's view, and a spotter is required when reversing in the loading bay. Forklift keys are signed out at the control desk and must be returned at the end of the shift.

## 6. Incident reporting

Every accident, near miss or damage to goods must be reported to the shift supervisor immediately. The written incident form must be submitted to the safety officer within 24 hours. Serious injuries must also be reported by telephone to the safety officer at once, and the safety officer notifies the national workplace inspectorate within 48 hours. The company does not penalise employees for reporting near misses.

## 7. Expenses

Employees are reimbursed for business expenses that were approved in advance by their manager. The daily meal allowance for business travel is 45 euros. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 140 euros per night. Any single expense above 75 euros requires a receipt and the approval of a department head. Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days, and payment is made in the next monthly payroll run, which is on the 25th of each month.

## 8. Remote work

Office staff, such as dispatchers and the finance team, may work from home up to 2 days per week. Warehouse and quayside employees cannot work remotely. Remote work must be agreed with the line manager, and employees working from home must be reachable between 09:00 and 15:00. The company provides a laptop and a headset, and reimburses 20 euros per month towards internet costs.

## 9. IT security

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and are changed every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication is required for the dispatch system and for e-mail. Employees must not install software that was not approved by the IT helpdesk, and lost devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 2 hours. The IT helpdesk is open from 07:00 to 19:00 on working days, and its telephone extension is 4417.
